I’m a husband, father, and the **Founder & Lead Consultant** at **Netrunnerz**, delivering enterprise IT, networking, and security solutions to schools, SMEs, and corporate environments. I believe in hard work, integrity, and lifelong learning — principles that have guided me from my earliest days to building and running a consultancy trusted by clients.

I was born and raised in South Africa during a time of significant hardship. My unemployed mother made handcrafted items to provide for our family. Growing up in this environment taught me resilience, creativity, and a deep appreciation for opportunities, no matter how small. These lessons shaped my values and work ethic: always act with integrity, and never compromise on quality.

At school, I excelled academically, consistently ranking as the top male student in my grade. I never missed a single day, driven by curiosity and a genuine love for learning. While university was initially out of reach due to financial constraints, I entered the workforce determined to grow, learn, and make an impact.

My career began as a **Finance Clerk** at a large South African arms manufacturer, and soon after I transitioned into IT roles in the energy sector. Over the years, I progressed from supporting IT services to managing major infrastructure and security initiatives, including **networks, servers, virtualization, backups, and system security**. I led projects such as designing and building new server rooms and establishing the IT environment for a company's head office, ensuring reliability, scalability, and security throughout.

I also **led and mentored small IT teams**, advised business unit managers on technology strategies, and promoted a culture of security awareness among staff. My work included internal audits, vulnerability assessments, and the creation of IT policies and procedures focused on best practices such as **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** and the **Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)**.

While working full-time, I achieved one of my lifelong goals: earning a **Bachelor of Commerce in Business Informatics (Cum Laude)** from the University of South Africa. This accomplishment reignited my passion for learning and inspired me to specialize further in cybersecurity. I pursued hands-on experience and advanced certifications, including CompTIA **Security+, Pentest+, CWES, CPTS, and OSCP**, which equip me to design IT systems that are secure, practical, and resilient in real-world environments.

In 2023, I founded **Netrunnerz (Pty) Ltd** to provide **enterprise IT, networking, and security consulting**. Through Netrunnerz, I **lead the design, deployment, and management of secure IT environments** for schools, SMEs, and corporate environments. While I personally oversee solution architecture and project delivery, I also work with **trusted specialist partners** where required, ensuring clients receive scalable, reliable solutions with a **single point of accountability**.

Beyond IT and security, I enjoy reading, gaming, spending time with my family, and gardening — pursuits that keep me grounded and balanced.
